ZOMBIE SHOOTERINGSIES (potato)

            

                1 Player: WASD to move, Mouse to aim & shoot, 1-8 to switch weapons.
                2 Players (Vertical Split-Screen):
                • P1 (Left Screen): WASD move | Mouse aim & Left-Click fire | 1-8 switch weapon
